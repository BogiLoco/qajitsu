`qj knowledge add|sync|remove|reset|reindex|list|search`: a per-project knowledge base with incremental sync by content hash, full or hybrid retrieval by size, embedding model checks, masking of secrets (including test account passwords from environment profiles) and a one-time confirmation for cloud embedding models. `qj status` shows its sources, mode and last sync.
